1. Getting Started – Fresh Install (No Sample Data)

This is a production system. Every new installation starts completely clean:

  1. Run python run.py from the project folder
  2. You are automatically taken to the Setup Wizard
  3. Create your first Branch (Name, Address, TIN, VMS UID — these are mandatory for FRCS VMS fiscalization)
  4. Create the first Administrator account
  5. Log in → immediately go to Settings and VMS Certificates to configure the system for your shop
No demo data is ever loaded. You build your own products, suppliers, debtors, tax rates and payment methods. This is how real shops start.

2. Shop Management

Set up your business foundation — branches, compliance, rules, and people. This is the "Shop Management" area (inspired by clean retail systems that treat shop setup as a distinct, critical workflow).

Branches & Multi-Location

Go to Settings → Branches. Every branch needs its own TIN and VMS UID from FRCS. The active branch in POS determines which certificate and receipt sequence is used.

VMS Certificates & Fiscal Mode (Critical)
  1. Go to VMS Certificates (Admin only)
  2. Upload your official FRCS .pfx or .p12 certificate (or use Simulation for testing)
  3. For each certificate choose VMS Mode:
    • Simulation — Fast local signing (great for training and testing)
    • Real VSDC — Live transmission to FRCS. Enter the sandbox URL https://vsdc.sandbox.vms.frcs.org.fj (or production when ready) and your Receipt Sequence prefix.
  4. Always click Test Signing after upload — it proves the private key works before you process real sales.
Tax Rates & Payment Methods

In Settings you fully control these — no hard-coded VAT. Default is G 12.5%; create Standard (G), Zero-Rated, Exempt, etc. Same for payments (CASH, CARD, MPAISA, ON_ACCOUNT, etc.). These flow into every calculation and every fiscal payload.

Receipt Design — Composer 2.5 Model (Visual)

Best option: Go to Receipt Designer (top nav or Settings → Open Visual Drag & Drop Composer 2.5). You can:

  • See all DB fields (Order, Branch, Debtor, Fiscal, Totals shortcuts...)
  • Drag + reorder into Header / Seller / Customer / Items / Totals / Fiscal / Footer
  • Add Free Text / Notes anywhere
  • Toggle 80mm vs A4 live preview (styled close to final output)
  • Save — the layout (Composer 2.5) now fully drives PDF receipts for both WeasyPrint and FPDF fallback paths

Legacy static layout in receipt.html is used when no composer layout is saved. Custom Header HTML in Settings still works on top.

Users, Roles & Permissions

Create staff with roles (admin, manager, staff). Granular permissions control who can do stock adjustments, issue credit notes, view daily sales summaries, manage debtors, view fiscal logs, etc. Permissions are set per user in Settings → Users.

3. Product Management

This is the heart of daily operations — adding products correctly, managing barcodes (including cartons/packs), pricing, and classification. The navigation now has a dedicated Products dropdown for these tasks (following practical retail product management flows).

Adding Products – Barcode is King
  • Barcode is now the compulsory field (primary key for scanning).
  • SKU is optional — if left blank the system auto-generates AUTO-{barcode}.
  • Fill Name, Department (recommended), regular Price, Cost, Reorder Level, Unit.
  • Optionally assign a Special Pricing Group for bundle/quantity deals.
Alternate Barcodes & Pack Quantities (Cartons, Packs, Boxes)

In the product edit form (or via the Manage button), add as many alternate barcodes as you need, each with its own Pack Quantity.

Example: Main barcode 123456 (single unit)
Alternate 789012 with Pack Qty = 6 → scanning 789012 in POS or Invoice Entry automatically adds 6 units.

This is perfect for cartons, sleeves, or wholesale packs. The movement history correctly records the actual units sold/received.

No Duplicate Barcodes

The system checks both the primary barcode and all alternate barcodes across every product. If you try to use a barcode that already exists you will see a clear popup: "This barcode is already used by: [Exact Product Name]". No silent failures.

Departments & Classification

Use Departments (under Products dropdown) for structured grouping: Groceries, Beverages, Tobacco, Hardware, etc. Select the department when creating/editing any product. Great for future reporting and filtering.

Pricing Power
  • Regular selling price (default for all branches)
  • Branch-specific overrides (click "Set price" on any branch column in Inventory)
  • Special Pricing Rules (create in the Special Pricing page) — quantity or bundle deals that auto-apply in POS with a visible badge. Click any cart item to see exactly which rule fired.
Bulk Tools

Excel Import (in Inventory): prepare columns SKU, Name, Barcode, Price, Cost, Department, ReorderLevel… and load hundreds of items in one go. Existing items (matched by SKU or Barcode) are updated safely.

Bulk Update: mass change prices, costs, reorder levels, or departments across many products.

Stock Visibility & Traceability

Inventory screen shows real-time on-hand per branch + on-order quantities. Every product has a History button that opens the complete Stock Movement Summary (every sale, goods receipt, adjustment, transfer, invoice entry, reversal — with before/after quantities, user, date and reference link).

4. Purchasing & Suppliers (Purchase Management)

Create supplier orders, receive goods (partial or full), and let stock update automatically with full audit. This matches the "Purchase Management System" concept — tightly connected to your product catalog and inventory.

Suppliers

Manage supplier records (name, contact, TIN if needed) from the Purchasing dropdown.

Purchase Orders

Create POs with multiple line items. Enter both Cost VEP (ex-tax) and Cost VIP (inc-tax) on every line — important for accurate accounting and later variance analysis.

Goods Receipt & Partial Receiving

From any PO, click Receive Goods. The screen shows exactly what is still remaining. Use "Receive All Remaining" for speed, or enter partial quantities. The system prevents over-receiving. Stock is increased instantly with a PURCHASE_RECEIPT movement record.

Administrators can reverse any Goods Receipt — stock is perfectly rolled back with a matching reversal movement.

Stock Take

Periodic full counts. Start a stock take → enter actual counted quantities → complete. Variances are recorded as ADJUSTMENT movements.

5. Sales, POS & Debtors

POS Terminal (Daily Heart of the Business)
  • Switch branches instantly at the top of the screen.
  • Search by barcode (primary or any alternate) or product name. Alternate barcodes with pack qty automatically add the correct number of units.
  • Select a Debtor → live current balance is fetched and shown in a panel.
  • Special pricing applies automatically. Click any line in the cart to see exactly which rule is active.
  • Multiple payment methods in one sale (split tender supported).
  • Every completed sale is fiscalized. The PDF receipt (80mm or A4) always shows full sales details (items, debtor snapshot, VEP/VIP totals, payment). The full official FRCS fiscal block (SDC No, signature, verification URL) only appears when VMS Real mode is active for the branch.
Debtors / On-Account Customers

Create and manage customers who buy on credit. When you select them in POS or Invoice Entry their current balance is shown live. The final fiscal receipt captures a full snapshot (name, TIN, phone, address, balance after this sale). Use the Debtors page "Record Payment" button to reduce balances (optional audit receipt). Credit notes/returns now also reduce linked debtor AR.

Rental Management (New)

Add Properties with barcodes (scannable like products). Add Tenants and assign to properties. Go to Rental → Rent Collection (Scan & Pay) — the interface is deliberately almost identical to the main POS (same cart, scan input, Amount Received + change, payment methods). Scan a property barcode to pull tenant + due rent, add charges or payments, complete to record. Tenant outstanding balances update automatically. See Rental → Properties / Tenants / History for management. Use Maintenance / Work Orders (under Rental) to log and track repairs per property, with costs and status updates. Residential = VEP, Commercial = VIP for rent tax treatment.

Invoice Entry (Supplier Invoice)

Found under the Purchasing dropdown → "Invoice Entry (Supplier Invoice)". Use this to record a supplier's invoice and add stock (never deducts, never fiscalizes):

  • Select Supplier + enter their Invoice Number + Payment type (for your records)
  • Add lines by product; enter Packs qty (e.g. 2 cartons). Pieces auto-calculated using the product's Pack Size (set in Inventory, e.g. 1 carton = 24 pcs)
  • Enter the supplier's unit cost VEP / VIP per piece (or let VIP auto-calc from VEP at 12.5% for UI); line tax and totals shown live
  • Creates a Goods Receipt (with supplier_invoice_no) + SUPPLIER_INVOICE StockMovement(s) + updates on-hand. Full audit, no VMS.
  • Pack support in Inventory: every product can have pack_size, pack_unit, pack_cost, and a default Supplier assignment.
Sales History & Receipts

Full list of all sales with filters. Click any sale to see details and regenerate the PDF receipt (with all VMS fiscal data, debtor snapshot, and line items).

6. Stock Control & Adjustments

Every single change to stock goes through the central Stock Movement ledger. Nothing is ever updated "silently".

  • Stock Adjustment (quick link in nav) — increase or decrease with reason. Creates ADJUSTMENT movement.
  • Transfers — move stock between your branches (two movements created: TRANSFER_OUT + TRANSFER_IN).
  • Stock Take — full count process with variance adjustments.
  • History button on every product in Inventory — the single source of truth for that item's life.
Movement types recorded: SALE, SUPPLIER_INVOICE, GR (goods receipt), ADJUSTMENT, STOCKTAKE, TRANSFER_IN/OUT, RETURN, REVERSAL, etc. (full immutable audit).

7. Reports & Business Insights

The Reports page gives you sales, stock, and compliance visibility. There is also a dedicated Daily Sales Summary (accessible via top nav or Reports) that shows sales grouped by date and payment method, with credit notes/returns clearly netted.

Access to specific reports is controlled by granular permissions.

8. VMS Fiscal Compliance & Logs

Every sale (POS or Invoice Entry) that is fiscalized produces a cryptographically signed invoice that is transmitted to FRCS VMS.

  • View transmission status on every sale detail page.
  • VMS Logs (Admin) — full history of attempts, responses, SDC invoice numbers, QR codes, and error details. Essential for troubleshooting with FRCS.
  • Simulation mode lets you test the entire flow without touching the live VSDC service.

9. Backup, Restore & Maintenance

From Settings (Admin) you can create a full database backup at any time and restore from a previous backup file. Use this before major changes or as part of your regular routine.

10. Troubleshooting & FAQ

Q: "I uploaded a PFX but Test Signing fails"
Double-check the passphrase. Re-upload with the exact password you used when exporting the PFX from FRCS. Always use the Test Signing button before going live.
Q: "Barcode already used" error when it shouldn't be"
The check covers both the main barcode field and every alternate barcode on every product. Use the History or search to find the conflicting item.
Q: "Stock is wrong after a goods receipt"
Check the movement history on the product. You can reverse the receipt (admin only) if it was entered incorrectly.
Q: POS is slow or database locked (Windows)
Only one instance of the app should run. Use Task Manager → Details → end all python.exe, then restart run.py.
Still stuck? Check VMS Logs for fiscal errors, or the console output of run.py for Python tracebacks. Most issues are configuration or duplicate data.
